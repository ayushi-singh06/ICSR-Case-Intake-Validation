ICSR Case Intake and Validation

Topic Area: Pharmacovigilance
Audience: Beginner
Product: Velunex (Training Fictional Product)
Objectives:

To convert de-identified adverse event data into an ICSR, determine case completeness, understand drug-event timeline and identify gaps in information.

Valid ICSR Elements

A valid ICSR will need to have:

1. Patient information
2. Reporter information
3. Suspected product
4. Adverse event

Conclusion for Validation:
All 4 cases have a suspected product and adverse event with enough information to determine a drug-event timeline. However the dataset does not provide any identifiable details related to the reporter so cannot be completely confirmed as having the minimum required for a valid ICSR without further follow up.
Priority for follow up required for PV-102 and PV-104 due to hospital visits meaning they are serious cases. They are also unexpected as shown by the fictional Velunex reference provided.
Causality:
There is a temporal relationship between using Velunex and developing the adverse events however there is no evidence to show that it was caused by Velunex. Additional clinical information would be needed and alternative causes considered.
