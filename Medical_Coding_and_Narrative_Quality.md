3. Narratives
PV-101
A 34 year old female from India reported experiencing nausea 3 hours after taking the fictional product Velunex. The outcome was recovered. The event was not serious and no concomitant information was reported. Dose, route, indication, precise date of exposure, date of event, treatment, medical history and reporter details were all not provided. There is no indication that the nausea was caused by Velunex.
Coding suggestion – Nausea
PV-102
A 61 year old male from India reported experiencing loss of consciousness 2 days after taking the fictional product Velunex. They were admitted to A&E and are recovering. Concomitant information was unknown. Dose, route, indication, precise dates, how long they lost consciousness for, diagnosis, investigations, treatment, medical history and reporter details were not provided. There is no indication of a cause.
Coding suggestion – Loss of consciousness
PV-103
A 27 year old female from the United Kingdom reported experiencing a mild rash 4 days after taking the fictional product Velunex. The outcome was recovered. The event was not serious and no rechallenge was reported. Dose, route, indication, precise dates, details of rash, treatment, medical history and reporter details were not provided. There is no indication that the rash was caused by Velunex.
Coding suggestion – Rash. The qualifier mild has been included.
PV-104
An unknown gender patient of unknown age from India reported experiencing appendicitis 3 weeks after taking the fictional product Velunex. They were hospitalised and had a recovered outcome. Medical history was incomplete. Dose, route, indication, precise dates, diagnosis information, treatment, concomitant medication and reporter details were not provided. There is no indication of a cause.
Coding suggestion – Appendicitis
